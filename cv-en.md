#### Responsibilities ####
- I design interfaces based on user scenarios, analytics, statistics, and metrics
- I work with complicated scenarios and pay attention to details
- I understand what can be sacrificed in an interface when development resources are limited
- Together with the UX researcher, I: formulate hypotheses, create usability testing scenarios, prepare interactive prototypes, participate in user interviews, draw conclusions based on research
- I write clear interface texts
- I propose improvements to existing solutions in the service. I openly point out critical issues, warn about potential future problems, and influence development plans.
- I explain and defend my ideas. I ask questions to better understand the other person. I recognize the importance of team communication and trust in design decisions
- I systematize and organize everything in my work. I ensure that prototypes are convenient not only for designers but also for the development team. If I see problems, I propose solutions
- I suggest improvements to development processes when I notice problems
- I mentor designers by sharing my experience and helping them grow
- I assist in hiring designers by reviewing resumes, evaluating portfolios, and conducting interviews

## Onboarding in Elba

<<Цвет>>
blue

<<Картинка>>
../img/portfolio/04-00-onboarding.png

<<Картинка мобильная>>
../img/portfolio/04-00-onboarding-mobile.png

<<Задача>>
Provide guidance to new users so they don’t feel lost in the new service and feel our support. Reduce the complexity of onboarding into the service.

<<Решение>>
I studied the experience of new users in our service. I realised that there are two groups of users. Developed a solution that solves the problems of both groups. Onboarding consists of two functional parts. The first part is navigational tooltips. They help guide users through the service and highlight the most important things. The second part is reference materials for users. They explain to newbies in accounting how to manage bookkeeping properly.
